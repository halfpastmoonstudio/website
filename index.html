About the studio

We like quiet nights, odd skies, and the little details you only notice when you stop and look twice.

Half Past Moon Studio is a new, small independent studio making games on Roblox. Our games are spooky but mild: atmosphere over gore and suspense over shock. Teens and grown-ups can enjoy the same scare, and nobody needs to look away.

We’re just getting started, and we’d rather show you than tell you.

What we believe

  • ☾

    Atmosphere over gore

    Dread comes from a hush, a shadow, a lamp that flickers once. Never from cruelty.

  • ✦

    Wonder over jump scares

    The best moment is the one where you lean closer, not the one where you flinch.

  • ◉

    Reward the second look

    Every detail is placed on purpose, so the curious and the careful always find a little more.
