Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026

Half Past Moon Studio ("we," "us") is a small independent studio that makes games on Roblox. This policy explains, in plain language, what information we handle on our website (halfpastmoonstudio.com), in our games on Roblox, and in the tools we use to post our own videos.

The short version

  • Our website has no accounts, no sign-up forms, no analytics, no ads and no cookies.
  • We don't knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13.
  • Roblox runs player accounts, chat and payments under its own policies. We don't get your password or payment details.
  • Our video tools only upload our own videos to our own YouTube and TikTok accounts. They don't collect or store anyone else's data.
  • Questions? Email hello@halfpastmoonstudio.com.

Our website

What we collect: nothing directly. The site has no accounts, forms, comment boxes, newsletters, analytics, tracking pixels, ad tags or cookies. Our fonts and images are served from our own site, not from third-party services.

Hosting: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may automatically log technical details such as your IP address when you visit, for security and operations. That is handled under GitHub's Privacy Statement; we don't receive or use those logs.

Email: if you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to reply or to send you the news you asked for, and we don't sell or share it. We keep emails only as long as needed to respond and keep a record of the conversation, and you can ask us to delete them at any time.

Our games on Roblox

Our games run on Roblox. Roblox Corporation handles player accounts, age settings, parental controls, chat, safety features and all Robux purchases under the Roblox Privacy and Cookie Policy and Roblox Terms of Use. We never see your Roblox password, real name, email, or payment details.

Like any Roblox game, ours save gameplay data through Roblox's own systems so your progress works, for example your Roblox user ID, username, in-game items, currency and progress. We may also use Roblox's built-in, aggregated game statistics (such as how many people played) to improve our games. We don't use this data to build profiles for advertising, and we don't ask players for personal information like names, emails, addresses or photos in our games.

Children's privacy (COPPA)

Some of our games are made for young kids. We take that seriously:

  • We don't knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13, on our website or in our games.
  • Kids' accounts, chat and purchases are managed by Roblox, including Roblox's parental controls and age-based safety settings.
  • Our videos made for kids are marked "Made for Kids" on YouTube, which turns off comments and personalized ads on those videos.
  • We don't target paid ads at children.

If you're a parent or guardian and think your child sent us personal information (for example, by emailing us), contact hello@halfpastmoonstudio.com and we'll delete it promptly.

Our social accounts

We post on YouTube and on TikTok (@halfpastmoonstudio). When you watch, like or comment there, YouTube (Google) and TikTok handle your data under their own privacy policies: Google Privacy Policy, TikTok Privacy Policy. We can see public comments and basic statistics those platforms show to every channel owner.

Our video upload tools (YouTube and TikTok APIs)

We built small internal tools that use the YouTube Data API and the TikTok Content Posting API only to upload our own videos to our own accounts. Only our studio signs in to these tools; they are not offered to the public.

YouTube. Our tool uses YouTube API Services and asks Google only for permission to upload videos to our own channel. By using it, we agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; see also the Google Privacy Policy. Half Past Moon Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The only Google data the tool keeps is the sign-in token for our own account, stored securely on our own computer so uploads keep working. We don't access, store or share any other user's data. You can revoke this tool's access at any time through Google's security settings.

TikTok. Our tool uses TikTok's draft upload feature: it sends a finished video to our own TikTok account's inbox as a draft, and we review, edit and post it ourselves in the TikTok app. It never posts automatically. The only TikTok data the tool keeps is the sign-in token for our own account, stored securely. We don't access, store or share any other TikTok user's data. Access can be removed in the TikTok app's security settings at any time.

Sharing and selling

We don't sell personal information, and we don't share it for advertising. We'd only disclose information if the law requires it.

Security

We keep what little we handle (mainly emails and our own sign-in tokens) in password-protected accounts and secure storage. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep data to a minimum so there is very little to protect.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to see, correct or delete any information you've sent us by emailing hello@halfpastmoonstudio.com. For Roblox account data, use Roblox's own settings or contact Roblox. If you live in a place that gives you extra privacy rights, email us and we'll honor them as required by law.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update it here and change the effective date. If a change is significant, we'll note it on our homepage.
