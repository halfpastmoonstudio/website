01 · The story

Why "Half Past Moon"?

The name comes from half past midnight, that quiet hour when the house is asleep and the best ideas finally show up. That's when this studio started, and it's still our favorite time to build.

We're small on purpose. We care about friendly worlds, fair play and games that are safe and fun for kids and teens.
