About the studio

Half Past Moon Studio is a small independent studio making games on Roblox. Our name comes from the hour when ideas show up: half past midnight, with the moon high overhead. It’s also the hour players stay up a little late for a game they love.

We don’t stick to one genre. Some of our worlds will be spooky, some cozy, some creative, and some made for a party. What ties them together is care: clear rules, fair play, details worth finding, and fun that teens and grown-ups can share.

We’re just getting started. We want to become a studio people trust for great games in any genre, and we’d rather earn that by shipping than by saying so.

Our latest game in development

Wren Hill

Clock in for the night shift at Wren Hill Observatory & Planetarium and report every anomaly before dawn.

The old observatory sits on a hilltop, watching the sky. Inside the dome and out across the grounds, as day turns to night, something up there has started getting things wrong. Your job is to notice.

Spooky, with mild scares and no gore.
